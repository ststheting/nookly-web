Privacy Policy for Nookly

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Nookly ("we," "our," or "the app") is a kitchen management app that helps you track pantry inventory, discover recipes, and plan meals. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (handled securely by our backend provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password).

Pantry & recipe data. Information you enter or generate while using the app, including:

  • Inventory items (name, category, quantity, storage location, expiration dates)
  • Recipes you save, generate, or import (including source URLs for imported recipes)
  • Meal plans and shopping lists
  • Chat messages you send to the in-app AI assistant

Photos. When you use the camera-scan feature to identify pantry items, the photo is sent to our AI provider (Google Gemini) for analysis and is not permanently stored by Nookly after processing, beyond any thumbnail you explicitly choose to keep.

Automatically collected information.

  • Device and app diagnostics (crash logs, performance data) if you have diagnostics sharing enabled at the iOS level

Expiry alerts are delivered as local notifications scheduled on your device. Nookly does not use remote push notifications and does not collect a push notification token.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:

  • Provide core app functionality (pantry tracking, recipe generation, meal planning)
  • Identify ingredients from photos you submit
  • Generate personalized recipe suggestions based on your pantry contents
  • Send expiry-alert notifications for items nearing their use-by date
  • Import recipe content from URLs you provide (websites, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok)
  • Maintain and improve the app's reliability and performance

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

Nookly relies on the following third-party services to operate:

ServicePurposeData shared
Supabase Authentication, database, file storage, backend functions Account info, pantry/recipe/meal-plan data
Google Gemini API AI-powered ingredient recognition and recipe generation Photos you scan, pantry contents, chat messages
YouTube Data API Fetching video metadata when importing a recipe from a YouTube link The YouTube URL you provide

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf. We only share the minimum data necessary for each service to perform its function.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual pantry items, recipes, or meal plans at any time within the app. To request full account deletion and erasure of your associated data, contact us at support@nookly.cc.

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all network requests and Row Level Security policies in our database so that users can only access their own data.

6. Children's Privacy

Nookly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at support@nookly.cc and we will delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@nookly.cc.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will indicate the date of the most recent update at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@nookly.cc.
